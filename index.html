How to play
  • Black moves first, then players alternate placing a stone on any empty intersection.
  • The first to make an unbroken row of five (across, down or diagonal) wins.
  • The host can change board size, line length and the overline rule in the lobby.
  • Play a friend, a spectator crowd, or the computer. Undo needs your opponent's OK.
